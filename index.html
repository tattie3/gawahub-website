⚗️ Chemistry

Complete chemistry revision guide

🔬 Topics Covered

Atomic Structure: Learn about electrons, protons, neutrons, and atomic models. Understand electron configurations and their properties.

Chemical Bonding: Master ionic, covalent, and metallic bonds. Understand electronegativity and bond polarity.

Acids & Bases: pH scale, neutralization reactions, buffers, and titration techniques.

Organic Chemistry: Hydrocarbons, functional groups, isomerism, and reaction mechanisms.

Thermochemistry: Enthalpy, entropy, Gibbs free energy, and equilibrium constants.

Periodic Table: Trends in atomic radius, ionization energy, electronegativity, and oxidation states.

⚡ Physics

Complete physics revision guide

🌍 Topics Covered

Newton's Laws: First, second, and third laws of motion. Force, mass, acceleration relationships.

Energy: Kinetic and potential energy, work-energy theorem, conservation of energy.

Waves: Transverse and longitudinal waves, frequency, wavelength, wave equation, interference, diffraction.

Electricity: Electric charge, current, voltage, resistance, Ohm's law, circuits, and magnetism.

Light: Reflection, refraction, lenses, mirrors, spectrum, and electromagnetic waves.

Thermodynamics: Heat, temperature, specific heat capacity, states of matter, and gas laws.
