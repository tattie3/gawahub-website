Syllabus Breakdown

Every Topic, Covered.

The full 2026–2028 Cambridge IGCSE Physics syllabus — tackled chapter by chapter with clear explanations and worked examples.

⚡
Chapter 1

Motion, Forces & Energy

Speed, velocity, acceleration, Newton's laws, momentum, work, power, and energy transformations — the backbone of all physics.

🌡️
Chapter 2

Thermal Physics

Kinetic model of matter, thermal expansion, specific heat capacity, latent heat, and the gas laws in action.

🌊
Chapter 3

Waves

Transverse and longitudinal waves, light, sound, reflection, refraction, diffraction, and the electromagnetic spectrum.

🔌
Chapter 4

Electricity & Magnetism

Circuit theory, Ohm's law, series/parallel circuits, electromagnetism, motors, generators, and transformers.

☢️
Chapter 5

Nuclear Physics

Atomic structure, radioactive decay, half-life, fission, fusion, and radiation safety — demystified.

🪐
Chapter 6

Space Physics

The Solar System, stars, galaxies, the Big Bang, and the expanding universe — physics at its biggest scale.
